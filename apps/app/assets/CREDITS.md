# Pictures

Every picture in this app comes from Elite Smile's own website (elitesmile.nl and arab.elitesmile.nl, October 2026):

- `brand/logo-light.png`, `brand/logo-dark.png`: the logo (the light version has "ELITE" recoloured navy for white backgrounds).
- `team/*.webp`: the six dentists' portraits from "Meet the Team of Professionals".
- `photos/clinic-chair.jpg`: the site's hero photo. `photos/kids.jpg`, `braces.jpg`, `crown-model.jpg`, `smile.jpg`: the pictures by the specialties.

The tooth mark drawn in the app (`packages/shared/src/brand.ts`) is traced from the logo.
